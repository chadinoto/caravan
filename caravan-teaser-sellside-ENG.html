The protocol

Send one request.
Receive every offer.

CARAVAN is a Request for Quote (RFQ) platform built for fresh produce. You post your supply — product, volume, quality class, delivery date — and send it simultaneously to all relevant buyers. Each responds with their best price. You see all bids in one view, choose the best, and the deal is confirmed and recorded.

The same protocol used in global financial markets to run competitive price discovery — now brought to fresh produce.
