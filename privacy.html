This notice explains what personal data CARAVAN collects through this website, why, and the rights you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to respond to you.

1. Who we are

CARAVAN [BV] (“CARAVAN”, “we”) is the controller of the personal data described here.

  • Registered office: [Registered address], Belgium
  • Company / VAT number: BE 0XXX.XXX.XXX
  • Contact: contact@caravan.trade

2. What we collect

When you submit the contact form on our website, we collect only:

  • Name — so we know who we are talking to;
  • Email address — so we can reply;
  • Message (optional) — any context you choose to share.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. See section 7 on local storage.

3. Why we process it, and our legal basis

We use this data solely to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss CARAVAN with you. The legal basis is your consent (given when you submit the form) and our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us. We will not use your details for unrelated marketing without asking you first.

4. Who else processes it

Form submissions are delivered to us through Formspree (Formspree, Inc.), acting as our processor. Your message may be transmitted to and stored on their infrastructure for delivery. See Formspree's privacy policy. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry only as long as needed to handle it and any follow-up conversation, and no longer than [24 months] unless an ongoing relationship requires otherwise. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email contact@caravan.trade.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

7. Cookies & local storage

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We store a single value in your browser's local storage — your language preference (EN/NL) — so the site remembers it on your next visit. It contains no personal information and is never sent to a server.

8. Changes

We may update this notice as CARAVAN develops. The “last updated” date above always reflects the current version.

9. Contact

Questions about your data? Email contact@caravan.trade.

Deze site gebruikt geen tracking- of advertentiecookies. We bewaren één waarde in de lokale opslag van je browser — je taalvoorkeur (EN/NL) — zodat de site die onthoudt bij je volgende bezoek. Ze bevat geen persoonsgegevens en wordt nooit naar een server gestuurd.
