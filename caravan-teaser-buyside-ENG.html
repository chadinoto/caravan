For fresh produce buyers

You sourced your volume.
You got one price.
Was it the best one?

In fresh produce, you almost never know. You call a trusted supplier, agree a price, and move on. But across the market that same day, other offers exist — sometimes better, sometimes significantly better. You'll never see them unless you ask everyone at once.

01
You negotiate blind

Your supplier knows the market. You know your budget. That asymmetry costs you on every deal you close.

02
Your network is your ceiling

You can only buy from who you know. When your regular supplier can't deliver, you're scrambling — not sourcing.

03
Sourcing takes hours you don't have

Phone calls. WhatsApp chains. Follow-ups. Every kilo of fresh produce you buy costs more time than it should.

The protocol

Send one request.
Receive every offer.

CARAVAN is a Request for Quote (RFQ) platform built for fresh produce. You send a single request — product, volume, delivery date, indicative price — to multiple suppliers simultaneously. Each responds with their best offer. You see all prices at once, choose the best, and the deal is confirmed and recorded.

The same protocol used in global financial markets to run competitive price discovery — now brought to fresh produce.

Het protocol

Stuur één aanvraag.
Ontvang elk aanbod.

CARAVAN is een Request for Quote (RFQ) platform gebouwd voor verse producten. Je stuurt één aanvraag — product, volume, leveringsdatum, indicatieve prijs — naar meerdere leveranciers tegelijk. Elk reageert met hun beste aanbod. Jij ziet alle prijzen in één overzicht, kiest het beste, en de deal wordt bevestigd en geregistreerd.

Hetzelfde protocol dat in wereldwijde financiële markten wordt gebruikt voor competitieve prijsvorming — nu beschikbaar voor de versmarkt.
